01 How a bot makes a call

Every crypto and FX bot runs the same loop. The model part only suggests; code owns the gate, the size and the limits.

  1. 📡

    State engine

    Polls public order books and trades, builds a compact snapshot. Causal clock: unclosed bars are never used, and anything from the future raises LookaheadError.

  2. 🧠

    Scorer

    Emits a typed decision schema: regime · direction · confidence · toxic_flow · setup_quality · risk_state. Default is a local, deterministic stats scorer.

  3. 🚦

    Gate

    Fires only if setup_quality ≥ 2, confidence > 0.80 and risk_state = safe. Confidence < 0.60 or a crisis regime means no entries and flatten.

  4. ⚖️

    Sizing

    Fractional Kelly after costs, hard-capped at quarter Kelly, divided by stop distance, then clipped by the risk layer.

  5. 🛡️

    Risk layer

    Checked before every order. A model can only make it stricter, never looser.

  6. 🧾

    Paper broker

    Simulated fills against the live public book with fees, spread and slippage. Misses and partials are logged.

  7. 🌙

    Nightly review

    P&L, hit rate, fees and Brier calibration. It proposes new parameters, but they are never auto-applied.

Tap a desk to filter, then hover or tap a bot to flip its card and see what it does.

Crypto pit $10,000 paper

Eight bots, one per Coinbase pair, reading public Coinbase data (no keys) every 10 s and scoring every 60 s. Spot rules: long only, no shorts. Stops at 1.5× the 24h vol move, targets at 2.5×, max hold 48 h.

FX desk $10,000 paper

Eight pairs picked by a ranking of data quality, cost vs volatility, regime clarity and event risk, with correlated pairs de-duplicated. They respect market hours (Sun 17:00 to Fri 17:00 New York), sit out −60/+30 min around high-impact news, and flatten before the Friday close. Leverage ≤ 5× gross.

Stock research desk $100,000 paper

Eight role bots that work like a research team: night-shift research, a morning desk, SEC EDGAR filings and XBRL facts, a bull vs bear debate, 1 to 5 ratings, and a ledger that grades every call. Tickets are paper and need your typed approval before they fill.
